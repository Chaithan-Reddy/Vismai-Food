🧈 Butter Chicken Recipe

🥣 Ingredients

For the Chicken Marinade:

  • 500g chicken (cut into pieces)
  • ½ cup curd (yogurt)
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

For the Gravy:

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 onions (chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Salt to taste

👩‍🍳 Steps to Follow

Step 1: Marinate and Cook the Chicken

  1. Mix all marinade ingredients and coat the chicken well.
  2. Rest for 30 minutes (or longer for more flavor).
  3. Cook the chicken in a pan or grill until it’s slightly charred and cooked through. Set aside.

Step 2: Prepare the Gravy

  1. Add onions and sauté until golden brown.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add tomato puree, red chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt.
  4. Cook until oil separates from the masala.

Step 3: Combine Chicken and Gravy

  1. Add cooked chicken pieces to the gravy.
  2. Pour in cream and crushed kasuri methi.
  3. Stir well and simmer for 5–10 minutes on low flame.

Step 4: Serve

  • Serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.
