🍛 Hyderabadi Biryani

🥣 Ingredients

For the Chicken Marinade:

  • 500g chicken (bone-in preferred)
  • 1 cup curd (yogurt)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies (slit)
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tsp biryani masala
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp fried onions (birista)
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint leaves
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

For the Rice:

  • 2 cups basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • 4 cups water
  • 2–3 cloves
  • 2 cardamoms
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp salt

For Layering:

  • 2 tbsp fried onions
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • Chopped mint & coriander (for garnish)

👩‍🍳 Steps to Follow

Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

  1. In a large bowl, mix chicken with yogurt, spices, herbs, and fried onions.
  2. Cover and marinate for at least 2–3 hours (overnight gives best flavor).

Step 2: Parboil the Rice

  1. Boil water with whole spices and salt.
  2. Add soaked rice and cook till 70% done (grains should be slightly firm).
  3. Drain and set aside.

Step 3: Layer the Biryani

  1. In a heavy-bottomed pot, spread a layer of marinated chicken.
  2. Add a layer of half-cooked rice on top.
  3. Sprinkle saffron milk, ghee, fried onions, mint, and coriander.
  4. Repeat layers if needed, ending with rice on top.

  2. Cook on high flame for 5 minutes, then low flame for 25–30 minutes.
  3. Switch off heat and let it rest for 10 minutes.

Step 5: Serve

  • Gently mix before serving so rice and chicken combine evenly.
  • Serve hot with raita or salad.
