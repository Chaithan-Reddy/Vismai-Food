🍖 Mutton Curry Recipe

🥣 Ingredients

For the Marinade:

  • 500g mutton (goat meat, cut into medium pieces)
  • ½ cup curd (yogurt)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

For the Curry:

  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 2 onions (finely sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water (as needed)

👩‍🍳 Steps to Follow

Step 1: Marinate the Mutton

  1. Mix mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and all the marinade spices.
  2. Cover and keep for 1–2 hours (or overnight in the fridge for better taste).

Step 2: Fry Onions and Masala

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a pressure cooker or heavy pot.
  2. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes and all the spice powders.
  5. Cook until tomatoes turn soft and oil separates.

Step 3: Add Mutton

  1. Add marinated mutton to the masala and mix well.
  2. Cook on medium flame for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Step 4: Pressure Cook

  • Add 1–1½ cups of water.
  • Pressure cook for 5–6 whistles (or until mutton is tender).
    1. If cooking in a pot, cover and cook on low flame for 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Step 5: Final Touch

  1. Once cooked, add garam masala and simmer for 5 more minutes.
  2. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.

Step 6: Serve

  • Serve hot with steamed rice, roti, or naan.
